# Client rubric C1-C8 (DZT) and routing

| # | Criterion | 5/5 looks like | Common failure | Redo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Character | friendly everyday people, natural skin and hands, the same face and outfit in every shot, matches the reference image | melted faces, extra fingers, glowing eyes, person changes between shots | 04_people (new reference) or 06_prompts (less motion) |
| C2 | Personalisation | name spelled exactly, avatar/photo and options exactly as the product preview, nothing extra printed | misspelled or mirrored name, wrong colour/animal, invented text, **portrait/avatar from the preview missing (name pattern only) = hard failure; in group shots any item showing another person's portrait or name, two names, or an invented name = hard failure (never an accepted risk)** | 05_personalize (wrong preview) or 06_prompts (clip ignores the preview) |
| C3 | Setting | lived-in home, natural light, mood as in the sample | hotel/studio look, surreal background, props from nowhere | 06_prompts |
| C4 | Hook | first 2-3 s show a clear relatable moment and the product/reveal; caption readable, above the TikTok UI zone | slow start, product invisible, caption cut off | 06_prompts (hook clip) / 08_assembly (caption) |
| C5 | Hook-product link | the product answers the hook situation | emotion mismatch, the product appears unrelated | 06_prompts |
| C6 | Product visuals | right product, right colours, rigid shape, readable artwork, consistent across shots, true size (a blanket held up by a child is taller than the child; towel-sized = deformed_product hard failure) | deformed sock/blanket, garbled text, product changes between shots | 06_prompts (closer, stiller product shot; "exactly as @Image2") |
| C7 | Highlight customisation | a close, steady shot of the personalized area for 2+ s | personalization only glimpsed | 06_prompts (add close-up) |
| C8 | CTA | end card with product name + "Personalize yours at macorner.co", disclaimer visible, no made-up prices | missing end card, wrong wording, prices shown without approval | 08_assembly |

Hard failures (never deliver): misspelled name, other brand/logo/account name, profanity, rude gesture, deformed product, wrong personalization (including a mirrored/swapped layout or a bust where the preview shows a full-body figure), platform UI, watermark (including AI-model marks), unsafe content involving children, cast count different from the sample, no audible track.

Caption rules (client feedback 20 Oct): 1-2 short lines, small, top band, never over faces or the personalized area, emoji where the sample uses them. Texture rule: the material and all-over pattern must match the product-page gallery.

Pass rule: every score >= 4 and no hard failure. Max 3 retries per item; then deliver the best attempt flagged "needs human review" with the reports attached.
